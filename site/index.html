Pool Controller Firmware

Bridge a Connect 10 pool controller onto your network. Flash it from this page — no toolchain required.

Not an official product. This is a hobby project, not connected to or supported by Fluidra, and it comes with no warranty or support. You are flashing your own hardware at your own risk.

Your browser can't talk to serial devices. Use desktop Chrome or Edge — Safari, Firefox and mobile browsers don't support Web Serial. You can always flash manually with esptool instead. This page has to be served over HTTPS to access the serial port.

What you need

  • A Waveshare ESP32-C6 Mini (or another ESP32-C6 board), ideally on the project PCB.
  • A USB-C data cable — plenty of cables are charge-only and will not enumerate a serial port.
  • Desktop Chrome or Edge.

Installing

  1. Plug the board into your computer over USB-C.
  2. Press Connect & Install and pick the board's serial port from the browser prompt.
  3. If the port isn't listed, hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT to force the bootloader, then try again.
  4. Choose Install and wait — the first flash takes a couple of minutes.

Reflashing a device that is already set up? Pick Install without erasing to keep its saved WiFi and MQTT settings, or erase to start clean.

After flashing

  1. Power the board from the pool bus (an RJ12 cable to either socket) or leave it on USB.
  2. Wait for the LED to turn purple — provisioning mode.
  3. On your phone, join the WiFi network POOL_AABBCC with password poolsetup.
  4. Open http://192.168.4.1 and choose your WiFi network.
  5. The device restarts and reappears at http://poolcontrol-AABBCC.local. Green LED means WiFi and MQTT are both up.

This is a one-time trip. Later releases install over the air from the device's own web page or from Home Assistant — you should not need to plug into USB again.
